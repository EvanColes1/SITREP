                Method

                How SITREP collects, tags and scores the data.

                Collection

                Every three hours the collector reads 35 news and research feeds, trying a backup address if a publisher blocks the main one. It removes duplicate stories by link and by headline, and keeps 14 days of history. Sanctions and energy data refresh once a day.

                Tagging

                Each story is matched against more than 250 theme keywords and 500 country, capital and leader names. Headline matches count double. Short acronyms such as US, UK and EU only match in capitals, so the word “us” is never read as the United States.

                Signals

                For every theme, region and country, SITREP compares the last 24 hours with the average day over the previous 13. A signal needs at least 3 stories and is ranked by the ratio (24-hour count + 1) ÷ (baseline + 1), which stops tiny numbers from producing huge ratios.

                Risk index

                A 0–100 score for each country, built from three parts:

                  Bands: Low under 25, Guarded 25–44, Elevated 45–64, High 65–79, Severe 80 and over. The index measures attention and activity in open sources. It is not a forecast.
